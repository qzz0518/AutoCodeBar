Adding an ignored app now uses a pop-up list, and the Settings window's buttons, switches and fields were tuned up.

- **Pop-up app picker**: "Add App" opens a "Running Applications" list with app icons; apps already on the ignore list are checked and greyed out; move with ↑↓, confirm with Return, close with Esc; "Choose from Finder…" stays at the bottom. Rows now show the app name with its bundle identifier underneath.
- **Consistent settings controls**: inline buttons share one height and a hairline border, and short labels no longer shrink; buttons ease down slightly when pressed and stay still with Reduce Motion on.
- **Redrawn switches**: the on side uses the brand teal with a white knob, so the state reads at a glance in Dark Mode, and toggling animates.
- **Details**: secondary text is darker, field borders are clearer and thicken while focused, and panes open at the top when you switch.
